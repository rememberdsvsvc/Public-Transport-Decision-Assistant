# DECO6500 Inquiry and Evidence Plan

## Leverage Point

The intervention acts when a passenger whose planned journey is disrupted reviews the impact and chooses whether to continue or take an alternative. If it works, the passenger can identify the relevant impact and make a more informed next decision. The team selected this point because the implemented prototype directly supports it; it does not attempt to operate services, provide live alerts, or change transport policy.

## Committed Questions

| Layer | Evaluation Question |
|---|---|
| 1.1 Interaction and interface | Can a first-time participant complete the disruption-information and option-selection flow without direct assistance? |
| 1.2 Prototype and technical | For fixed simulated journeys, does the prototype consistently carry the selected journey and correctly show status, impact, and selectable options? |
| 2.1 In-context use | In a time-limited disrupted-trip scenario, which information do participants use or overlook before choosing an option? |
| 2.2 Human needs | Does the information support confidence in a travel decision, and what uncertainty remains? |

Record for A3: operator/service consequences, data governance, accessibility equity, and long-term transport outcomes. Do not claim that A2 answers these questions.
