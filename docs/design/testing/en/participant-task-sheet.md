# Prototype Evaluation Tasks

Use the prototype in the way that feels natural. There are no right or wrong answers. You may ask about the scenario, skip a question, or stop at any time.

## Task 1

You are planning a public-transport trip. Use the prototype to find a journey matching the trip you intend to take.

## Task 2

A disruption has affected part of that journey. Show what you think has changed and what it means for your trip.

## Task 3

You still need to reach your destination. Use the available information to decide what you would do.

## Task 4

Complete the prototype's final feedback step as you normally would.
