# Testing Contingency Plan

## Incomplete or Simulated Prototype

The app uses local SQLite simulation data. Tell participants: “The disruption and journey data are simulated for this prototype.” Test only to the highest working fidelity. Do not imply a real journey search, live alert, or external submission occurred. Record the limitation.

## Other Conditions

- **Only general users:** Run the core usability test; do not ask them to approve transport operations. Record the Group B evidence gap.
- **Ten-minute version:** 1 minute consent and think-aloud; 3 minutes T1; 3 minutes combined T2/T3; 2 minutes T4 or final confidence/feedback; 1 minute close. Prioritise T1-T3.
- **Remote test:** Participant controls their device and shares the screen; moderator observes and records timing; record only with consent.
- **Technical failure:** Record it separately from user error. Do not coach around a broken feature. Mark the task invalid if it cannot be attempted and reschedule where practical.
- **Very small sample:** Report qualitative formative patterns only; do not claim statistical validation.
- **Unclear scenario wording:** Clarify the real-world scenario neutrally without naming interface actions; record whether confusion remains after clarification.
