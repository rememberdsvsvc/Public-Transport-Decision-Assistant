# Moderator Script

## Before Session

- Open the prototype at the home screen and confirm the correct build and device.
- Prepare the [session template](test-session-template.md), a participant code, and recording setup if consented.
- Do not enter names or identifying information in repository notes.

## Opening and Consent

“Thank you for helping. We are testing this prototype, not you. There are no right or wrong answers. Please say what you are thinking, and you may skip or stop at any time. Do you agree to participate?”

“May we record your screen and voice for research notes? This is optional and separate from participation.” Record Yes or No.

Think-aloud example: “I am looking for information about delay, so I would try this.”

## Pre-test, Tasks, and Closing

Ask: “How often do you use public transport?” and “What would you normally do if a planned service were disrupted?” Read each scenario in the [participant task sheet](participant-task-sheet.md) verbatim. Do not name controls, turn labels into instructions, or explain the recommended option. After T2/T3 ask: “What did you expect?” and “How confident are you from 1 to 5?”

If stuck, use: silent observation; “What are you looking for right now?”; “What would you expect to happen here?”; one neutral prompt (“Please continue in the way that feels most natural”); direct help only if needed, recorded with the task result.

Ask after the test about the easiest and most confusing parts, missing information, barriers to use, difference from normal practice, and one improvement. Close with: “Thank you. We will analyse de-identified notes alongside other sessions to improve the prototype; this session does not evaluate you.”
